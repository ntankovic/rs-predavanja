# Illustration plan

All figures are original vector drawings generated by `assets/draw_diagrams.py` in the Lecture 01–03 card style (Croatian `*.hr.svg` for slides, `*.hr.pdf` for the chapter). English variants follow after Croatian review.

| Figure | Teaching purpose | Source or original drawing | Chapter/slide placement | Required steps or states | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `code-image-container` | Code → image → image registry → containers; image registry vs service registry | Original; definitions from Docker overview | Chapter "Primjer"; slide before Swarm | build, push, pull/run; one image, many containers | Croatian drafted |
| `swarm-overview` | Swarm keeps a desired state; one task = one container with its own address | Original; roles from Docker key concepts | Chapter "Primjer"; slide 5 | Manager, three nodes, three replicas | Croatian drafted |
| `swarm-reschedule` | A failed node's task is rescheduled elsewhere with a new address | Original; behaviour from Docker key concepts | Chapter "Primjer"; slide 6 | Node 2 down; new replica on node 3 with a new address | Croatian drafted |
| `name-address` | One name, several changing addresses; identifiers per instance | Original | Chapter "Ime, adresa i identifikator"; slide 7 | Failed/moved instance marked | Croatian drafted, PDF+HTML checked |
| `dns-tree` | Name space is a tree; resolver asks name servers and caches | Original; roles from RFC 1034 | Chapter "DNS"; slide 8 | Names and steps marked illustrative | Croatian drafted, PDF+HTML checked |
| `ttl-stale` | Cached address stays stale up to one TTL after a move | Original | Chapter "Predmemoriranje i TTL"; slide 9 | Lookup, move, failures, expiry, new lookup | Croatian drafted, PDF+HTML checked |
| `discovery-modes` | Client-side vs server-side discovery | Original; patterns from Richardson | Chapter "Na klijentu ili preko usmjernika"; slide 13 | Who queries the registry, who picks the instance | Croatian drafted, PDF+HTML checked |

| `dns-ttl.js` (interactive) | TTL trade-off: failed calls after a move vs DNS queries; re-resolve on failure | Original | Slide 10 (HTML only; chapter uses `ttl-stale`) | TTL presets incl. 0 and 300 s; client count; refresh on failure | Croatian drafted, browser-checked |
| `lease-registry.js` (interactive) | Service registry with leases: TTL and heartbeat interval vs removal delay, failed requests and false removals; heartbeats drawn as ♥ | Original | Slide 16 (HTML only; chapter uses `lease-timeline`) | Crash of B at 15 s; optional 8 s pause of C; renewal ≥ TTL warning | Croatian drafted, browser-checked |
